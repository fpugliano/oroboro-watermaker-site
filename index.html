What it is

A 220V AC marine reverse osmosis watermaker assembled from generic industrial and semi-industrial components, targeting a significant cost reduction versus branded units while maintaining performance and reliability.

That is the honest one-sentence description. A seawater pump, a membrane, a pressure vessel, some valves, and a motor controller — all sourced direct from the factories that make them, with no brand markup in between. Assembled, tested, and supported by two sailors who have done 30,000 miles across 4 continents and 2 oceans and know exactly what needs to work when you are far from a marina.

Your own watermaker changes everything. Drop anchor in a bay. Make 60 litres in an hour. Move on. Stay out as long as you want.

The price is wrong
Marine watermakers (reverse osmosis systems for boats) are sold at a massive premium by OEM brands (Spectra, Katadyn, Schenker, Hhyline). A typical 60–100 L/h unit retails for €4,000–8,000+, despite being built from industrial components that can be sourced for €1,000–2,000.

This project assembles a performance-equivalent watermaker from direct-sourced industrial components: Italian HP pump bought factory-direct, a generic or custom-fabricated membrane vessel, commodity RO membrane and pre-filters, and commercial SS316L HP valve components.
Built for big yachts and deep pockets. Not anymore.
Most marine watermakers are engineered for US 60Hz power and carry price tags to match — designed for owners with a yacht manager to handle the paperwork. Run one from a European inverter and you lose 16% of output permanently, with no fix. And when something breaks in Paros or Leros? Proprietary parts, from one supplier, shipped from overseas.

For European boats

Full output from your inverter

Most watermakers on the market are engineered for US 60Hz power. Europe runs at 50Hz — and that mismatch quietly steals 16% of your output, permanently. Oroboro uses a VFD to drive the motor at full 60Hz from any European inverter. Full performance from the power you already have.

Italian engineering

Italian pump — zero oil changes

An Italian-made ceramic triplex plunger pump from Modena. Water-lubricated ceramic plungers — no oil to change, no crankcase to service, nothing to forget. The same pump family used in professional RO systems worldwide. Built to outlast the boat.

☀️
Aegean summer ready

Make water while the sun shines

Dial the VFD down to 30–35Hz while you're at anchor. The system draws ~700W from the battery bank through the inverter — about 60A at 12V or 30A at 24V. A 400W solar array running 5 hours in a Med summer produces roughly 1,700 Wh — enough to cover 2 hours of watermaking at 30Hz. That's 60–70 litres of free water, in a bay, no engine running.

The parts

Off-the-shelf. Industrial grade. Global stock.

Every component is a standard catalogue part from an established manufacturer. Nothing proprietary. Nothing that requires a specialist to source or replace.

Motor
Industrial 1.1kW three-phase motor. Runs at full rated speed via the VFD, driven by your boat's 230V inverter.
Variable Frequency Drive
Adjustable frequency drive — single-phase 230V in, three-phase 60Hz out. Soft start. Adjustable 30–60Hz for solar-only running.
High Pressure Pump
Italian ceramic triplex plunger pump from Modena. Water-lubricated, no oil, no maintenance schedule.
Membrane Vessel
FRP 2540 format — 1,000 PSI rated. Holds any standard 2.5" × 40" seawater RO membrane element.
HP Needle Valve
SS316L high-pressure needle valve, 344 bar rated, 3/8" BSPP ports. Controls system pressure and output flow.
Pressure Gauge
Glycerine-filled 0–160 bar gauge. Reads operating pressure in real time — the primary instrument for setting and monitoring the system.
Flow Meter
Inline on permeate outlet. Reads L/min in real time. Tracks membrane health and confirms rated output.
TDS Meter
Hand-held digital probe. Measures permeate salinity in ppm. Target: under 500 ppm from open ocean seawater.
Boost Pump
12V DC self-priming pump. Pulls seawater through the pre-filters and feeds the HP pump inlet at the right pressure.
Pre-Filter Housings
Big Blue 10" × 4.5". Two stage: 20µm then 5µm wound polypropylene. Protects the HP pump and membrane.

The name

Oroboro.
The eternal cycle.

ουρόβοροςThe ouroboros is an ancient Greek symbol — a serpent eating its own tail, forming a perfect circle. It represents the eternal cycle: self-renewal, endless return, the thing that feeds itself forever.

We chose it because a watermaker is the closest thing to that eternal cycle that exists aboard a boat. The sea is infinite. Our system draws from it as long as you sail. The water you make eventually returns to the sea. The cycle closes, and begins again.

It is also the simplest expression of what independence at sea actually means: not needing the shore for something as fundamental as drinking water. The serpent feeds itself. So do you.
