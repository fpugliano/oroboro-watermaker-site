You know the scene

You've watched what happens on Greek town quays
when the meltemi blows.

35 knots over the stern. Boats stern-to, ropes crossing everywhere, bumpers flying. Someone dragging. Someone shouting. And you're there — in the middle of all of it — because you need to fill water tanks.

And the water you get? Half the time it tastes of salt. Pipes that feed straight off a municipal cistern, in a port that's right on the sea. You fill up, pay for a berth, navigate the chaos — and you're still not sure what's actually going in your tanks.

Your own watermaker changes everything. Drop anchor in a bay. Make 60 litres in an hour. Move on. Stay out as long as you want.

The price is wrong
A marine watermaker costs €4,000–8,000 from a brand name. The components — the Italian pump, the FilmTec membrane, the SS316L fittings — cost €1,200–2,000 sourced direct. You are paying €2,000–6,000 for the packaging.
Most watermakers weren't designed for Europe
The major brands are engineered for US 60Hz power. Run one on a European inverter and you lose 16% of your output — permanently, with no fix. And when something breaks in Bodrum? Proprietary parts, from one supplier, shipped from overseas.
Oroboro is built differently
A VFD drives the motor at full 60Hz from any European inverter. Ham-Let valves, Parker fittings, FilmTec membranes — standard industrial parts stocked by distributors from Athens to Palma.

⚡
For European boats

Full output from your inverter

Most watermakers on the market are engineered for US 60Hz power. Europe runs at 50Hz — and that mismatch quietly steals 16% of your output, permanently. Oroboro uses a VFD to drive the motor at full 60Hz from any European inverter. Full performance from the power you already have.

🔩
Italian engineering

AR Italian pump — zero oil changes

The Annovi Reverberi JRA2.2 is a ceramic triplex plunger pump from Modena, Italy. Water-lubricated ceramic plungers — no oil to change, no crankcase to service, nothing to forget. The same pump family used in professional RO systems worldwide. Built to outlast the boat.

☀️
Aegean summer ready

Make water while the sun shines

Dial the VFD down to 30–35Hz while you're at anchor. The motor draws ~55–65A at 12V and a 400W solar array covers most of that. On a Med summer day with 5+ hours of sun, you produce 150–175 litres and barely touch your battery bank. Free water, in a bay, no engine running.

🌍
Serviceable anywhere

Fix it in Bodrum. Or Lagos. Or anywhere.

Ham-Let high-pressure valve. Parker SS316L fittings. Dow FilmTec membrane. Schneider Electric VFD. Every component is an industrial catalogue part — stocked by distributors in Athens, Palma, Marseille, and beyond. Nothing that requires a call to one specific overseas supplier.
